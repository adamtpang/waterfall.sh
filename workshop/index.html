How picks earn a place

Four ranking gates.

A popular tool can still lose if it creates more context, permission, billing, or maintenance cost than useful work. skills.sh installs and mcp.directory discovery are signals, not quality or security verdicts.

01 · task fit

A named recurring job, not a generic promise to make agents better.

02 · evidence

Official sources, dated benchmarks, or observed local use.

03 · negative case

An honest reason to keep it off or choose something else.

04 · safe source

No copied secrets, machine config, or unlicensed third-party code.
