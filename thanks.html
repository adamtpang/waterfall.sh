Paid. Now set up your board.

Thank you. Stripe emails your receipt. The board runs on your own computer with your own model keys, so setup takes three commands.

1. Install

Needs Python 3 and git. The installer clones the project, installs the waterfall command, and is safe to re-run.

curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adamtpang/waterfall.sh/main/install.py | python3

2. Mark this computer as licensed

waterfall license paid

3. Open your board

waterfall remote open

The board opens at http://127.0.0.1:7910 on this computer. Add your OpenRouter key and your Claude, Grok, or Codex access in its settings. waterfall.sh never marks up tokens.

Need help?

Reply to your Stripe receipt or use the contact page. Bugs and questions can also go to the GitHub issues.
